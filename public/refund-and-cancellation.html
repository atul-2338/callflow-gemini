2. The 3-day trial

New businesses can start a 3-day trial for $0. The trial opens the CallFlow workspace on a local forwarding number and is limited to 5 captured leads per day and the AI minutes included with the trial. No payment method is required to start, and we do not charge you during the trial.

  • If you do not subscribe, the trial simply ends. There is nothing to cancel and no automatic charge.
  • If you subscribe to Solo, billing starts when the paid subscription begins — not retroactively for trial days.
  • Trial allowances (daily lead cap, included minutes and 3-day length) reset or expire with the trial and do not carry over as credits.
No surprise charges: CallFlow never converts a trial into a paid subscription without a completed Dodo Payments checkout.

3. Solo subscription and billing

The Solo plan is [INSERT SOLO PRICE] per month (currently $99/month), billed monthly in advance. Payments, receipts and payment-method updates are handled by Dodo Payments; CallFlow does not store your full card details.

  • Your billing date is the date of your first paid subscription and repeats monthly.
  • Each paid period includes the plan’s AI call minutes and features as shown at checkout.
  • If a payment fails, the subscription may move to past due or be suspended until the payment method is updated.
  • Minutes and usage are recorded in your workspace under Settings → Plan & minutes.

4. How to cancel

You can cancel your Solo subscription at any time:

  1. Open the Workspace → Settings → Plan & minutes and use the subscription management option to open the secure Dodo billing portal; or
  2. Email [INSERT BILLING EMAIL] from the email address on the account and ask us to cancel.

Cancel before your next billing date to avoid the next monthly charge. Cancellation stops future renewals; it does not automatically refund the current period. Your workspace and AI call answering remain active until the end of the period you already paid for, then the account moves to a cancelled state.

Keep your confirmation. Ask for or save the cancellation confirmation. If you cancel close to a billing date, contact us promptly so we can verify the timing.

5. Refunds

Because CallFlow provides an actively delivered service (telephony, AI call handling and workspace access), subscription fees are generally non-refundable once a billing period has started. However:

  • We will refund a charge made after a valid cancellation was confirmed, if the charge should not have occurred.
  • We will correct duplicate charges, unauthorized charges reported promptly, or billing errors caused by us.
  • If the service was materially unavailable for a significant part of a paid period and we cannot remedy it, contact us — we may issue a pro-rated credit or refund at our discretion.
  • Refund requests made within [INSERT REFUND WINDOW] of the charge (for example, 7 days) will be reviewed individually.
  • Nothing in this policy limits rights you have under applicable consumer protection law.

Approved refunds are returned to the original payment method through Dodo Payments. Card refunds typically appear within 5–10 business days, depending on your bank. Taxes and currency conversion differences are governed by the payment provider and local rules.
